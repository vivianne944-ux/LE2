Microbiology — Multi-Year Repeat Index

Virology · Mycology · Patient Safety · Emerging & Re-emerging Infections — 12 uploaded exam papers, 2020A to 2028. Year filter scoped to 2026–2028.

What this is. Built to surface what the examiners keep asking rather than to archive papers chronologically. Every repeat family shows each year's version separately, with a rationale for every single option — distractors included, because a distractor in one year is frequently the keyed answer in another.

Topics come from the lecture PDFs in the project (RNA Viruses 3, RNA Viruses 4, Mycology 1, Mycology 2, Patient Safety, Emerging & Re-emerging, Lab: AFB/PPD, Lab: Hepatitis Titers) plus the printed coverage tables of the 2026 and 2028 papers. Exam subjects with no lecture in the project — bacteriology, immunology, vaccinology, microbiota, PCR/lab methods — are filed under Uncategorized, never folded into the nearest topic.

Year filter is 2026–2028 only, as requested. Older papers (2020A, 2020B, 2021, 2022, 2023 SE, 2023 LT, 2024 UT5, 2024 LE3, 2025) are still fully used as evidence inside the families and the radar — they are just not offered as filter values.
