Microbiology LE2 Repeat Index

This site groups past questions by what examiners keep asking rather than listing every exam in order. Every answer choice has its own rationale, so each distractor doubles as review, and every question is labeled against your current coverage.

How to read this page
Direct / near repeat the examiner has literally asked this before, with at most small wording or choice changes.
Same concept, new framing different stem, same rule needed. Inverted / choice variant the relationship is reversed, flipped between TRUE and NOT, or a past answer returns as a distractor.
Uncategorized the tested concept sits outside Modules 18–22 and AFB & PPD. Items from other lectures that the current transes do cover are labeled with the covering topic and carry a coverage note.
⚠ Source flag a key that conflicts with its own rationale, a typo, a reconstructed choice order, or a cross-source disagreement. Keys are always shown exactly as printed.
Standard reference marks the few sentences not found in the uploaded exams or project transes, added so every choice still has a usable explanation.
