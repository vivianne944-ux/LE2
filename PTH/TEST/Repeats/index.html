Every repeat family is filed under the project lecture that actually covers it. Families with no matching lecture in the project are filed under Uncategorized.

What this is. Not a chronological dump of every question. These are the repeat families — the concepts the examiners have come back to across year levels — with each year's version kept separate so you can see how the framing changed, plus a rationale for every answer choice so the distractors teach you something too.
Built from the uploaded examination feedback PDFs. Answer keys are reproduced exactly as the source PDFs give them — where a key looks inconsistent with its own rationale or with another year, the discrepancy is flagged inside the family rather than quietly corrected. Study rationales are grounded on the exam rationales themselves and on the lecture PDFs in the project.
