1 · How to write the prescription

The 4 steps

  • Step 1: Where is the infection?
    • Bladder only (cystitis)
    • Kidney (pyelonephritis)
    • Skin (impetigo)
    • Throat (tonsillopharyngitis)
  • Step 2: Find the trap clue
    • Pregnant · child · elderly
    • G6PD
    • Allergy, and how bad it was (mild rash vs anaphylaxis)
    • Arrhythmia · tendon tear · kidney disease
    • Vomiting (cannot take oral)
  • Step 3: Cross out forbidden drug groups (section 2)
  • Step 4: Pick the drug and write every part (section 3)

The template

Name: ____________________ Age/Sex: ______ Address: _________________ Date: _________ ℞ [Generic name] [dosage form] [strength] Dispense No. ___ Label: Take ___ [tablet/capsule/mL] by mouth every ___ hours for ___ days. [food instruction if any] (Signature) ______________, M.D. Clinic address License No. ________ PTR No. ________

Parts they check

  • Superscription: name, age, sex, address, date
  • ℞ symbol
  • Inscription: generic name + form + strength
    • Example: "Cefuroxime tablet 500 mg"
  • Subscription: Dispense No. = total units for the whole course
  • Signa (Label): how many, route, how often, how long, food rule
  • Prescriber: signature, name + M.D., address, License No., PTR No.

Counting (Dispense No.)

  • Formula: doses per day × number of days
  • Every 12 h × 7 days = 14
  • Every 12 h × 10 days = 20
  • Once daily × 10 days = 10
  • Once daily × 3 days = 3
  • Every 6 h × 7 days = 28
  • Every 6 h × 2 days = 8

Syrup math

  • mL per dose = mg per dose ÷ mg per mL
  • 250 mg/5 mL = 50 mg per mL
  • 125 mg/5 mL = 25 mg per mL (double the mL)
  • Shortcut: at 50 mg/kg/day with 250 mg/5 mL
    • mL per day = weight in kg
    • mL per dose = weight ÷ doses per day
  • Bottles = total mL ÷ bottle size → always round up
